Copyright Policy

How we handle copyright infringement claims and takedown requests.

1

Our Policy

Bantu Stream Connect respects the intellectual property rights of others and expects creators using the Platform to do the same. If you are a copyright owner, or authorized to act on one's behalf, and believe that content on Bantu Stream Connect infringes your copyright, you can submit a takedown request using the form below.

We review every submission and, where the claim is valid, remove or disable access to the reported content. Creators found to be repeat infringers may have their content removed and their accounts terminated.

We do not yet have a formally registered DMCA designated agent. Until one is registered, copyright requests are handled by our support team at support@bantustreamconnect.com.

2

Submit a Takedown Request

Request submitted

3

Counter-Notifications

If your content was removed and you believe it was removed in error, you can email support@bantustreamconnect.com with a description of the content, its original location on the Platform, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the courts of Johannesburg, South Africa to resolve the dispute. We'll review counter-notifications and may reinstate content where appropriate.
